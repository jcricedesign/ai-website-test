Image system

Use a small set of fixed ratios so business pages and directory cards stay consistent even when source photography varies.

Business directory card4:5 portrait1200 × 1500 px preferred
Business page hero16:9 landscape1920 × 1080 px preferred
Supporting business images4:3 landscape1600 × 1200 px preferred
Building / leasing photography3:2 landscape1800 × 1200 px preferred

Implementation rules

  • The container determines the crop, not the photograph. Use fixed aspect-ratio containers with object-fit: cover.
  • Default to centered crops. Use per-image object-position only when a face, product, or focal point needs adjustment.
  • Ask for the highest-resolution original available rather than screenshots or pre-cropped images.
  • Avoid source images with text baked into them.
  • One strong source photo can be reused in multiple contexts through different crops.
  • Do not resize the interface to accommodate inconsistent photography; normalize photography to the interface.
