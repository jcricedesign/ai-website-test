How much Barbergame?

Three deliberately different starting points using the same Chop It Up brand and barber roster. Five improves the existing website. Eight reorganizes the experience around barbers and availability. Ten treats the client–barber relationship as the product.

5 — Focused website

Recognizable barbershop site. Less duplication, clearer hierarchy, stronger booking.

8 — Barber-centered

People, specialties and live availability become the primary way into the shop.

10 — Relationship-first

Returning clients rebook almost immediately; discovery becomes the secondary path.

5

Refine

Keep the familiar site model, but make the mobile hierarchy useful and stop repeating content just to fill pages.

What this pass is testing

All three directions use the same underlying content. The variable is focus: at 5, Chop It Up is still primarily a website; at 8, the barber becomes the center of discovery; at 10, the system recognizes an existing client relationship and gets out of the way. Real haircut portfolio imagery is now included in the barber-centered direction so the work itself can function as proof, not decoration.
