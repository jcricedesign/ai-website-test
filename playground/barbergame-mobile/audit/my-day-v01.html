What deserves the barber's attention now?

A working audit of My Day as BarberGame's temporal spine. This is not a UI specification. It is a place to test the product model before systemizing the interface.
Central hypothesis
BarberGame organizes the barber's attention around Now. My Day operates the present. Business configures the future. Stats interprets the past. Other information enters the working day only when time, importance, consequence, and actionability make it relevant.

The working-day cycle

Select a moment. We are testing what comes forward, what remains available, and what disappears.

Attention is a budget

Internal design states, not necessarily language shown to barbers.

Spotlight

Act now

Rare and consequential. Arrival, cancellation, lateness, conflict, or another event with meaningful action now.

Daylight

Operate now

The calm working field: current state, what is next, open time, breaks, and enough context to keep moving.

Starlight

Remember for later

Worth preserving but not worth interrupting. The system remembers so the barber does not have to.

Starlight → Daylight → Spotlight
Attention is contextual. “Tomorrow's hours aren't configured” can be Starlight during a packed day, Daylight after the last client, and potentially Spotlight when missing hours are about to prevent bookings.

Interaction doctrine

The barber should never have to understand these rules. They should feel the result.

Questions to break the model

Resolve these through scenarios and prototype behavior, not theory alone.
