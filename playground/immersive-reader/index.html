The things we keep

An original placeholder poem · 1 minute

I kept the small things.
The key without a door,
a pencilled name inside a book,
the stone you brought from shore.

Not knowing what to call them,
I gave them all a drawer.
And every time I moved the house,
I moved them once more.

Today the light fell sideways
across that little collection.
Nothing asked to be useful.
Nothing needed correction.

I held the stone a moment.
The room was very still.
Some things outlive their stories.
Some things tell them still.
