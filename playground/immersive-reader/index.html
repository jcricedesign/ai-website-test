The things we keep

A small poem in a quiet reading space. Adjust the typography, listen to the whole piece, or hear a single line.

I kept the small things.
The key without a door,
a pencilled name
inside a book.

Sentence review

Move through the writing with your keyboard. Listen, speak a replacement, and consider it alongside the original.

Nothing asked to be useful.
Nothing needed correction.
I held the stone a moment.
