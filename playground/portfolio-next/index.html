Pass 1 / Information architecture

Four destinations, each with a different job.

01

Selected Work

What feels worth seeing now. Editorial and changeable: Human Interfaces, BarberGame, Mozilla, Bothell Suites, or whatever best represents the current story.

02

0→1

The commercial proposition: ambiguity, systems, milestones, product direction, and turning fragmented parts into something coherent and real.

03

Playground

The working floor: prototypes, research, vocabulary, client work, experiments, development handoffs, and eventually private client access.

04

About

Career history, chronology, résumé, contact, and enough context to understand the person behind the work without making career history the site’s organizing principle.

What changes

Career no longer needs to be a top-level destination. Its useful material can move into About. Selected Work becomes intentionally editorial rather than a fixed canonical portfolio. Playground becomes the living body of work rather than a miscellaneous bucket.

The next pass is subtraction and reclassification: decide what stays, what moves, what combines, and what can disappear.
