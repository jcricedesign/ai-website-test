Content foundations

Use a few reusable objects: post, project, archive item, service, contact, private reference. Each needs a title, body or source, author, contributors, dates, revision history, and publication state. Archive items also retain original date, format, and provenance.

Visibility and publication state are separate: a private item must never become public just because its status changes. Private content belongs in protected storage, outside the public repository. Unannounced project material is held until explicitly cleared.

Complete journeys, including recovery

Read and connect

Home → Parkinson’s → Article → Related writing or Contact / Follow.

A direct article link works without visiting Home. Empty sections explain what is coming; contact methods remain available. Reading controls preserve position.

Geof publishes

Workspace → Capture a thought → Review exact wording and audience → Publish → View on site.

Capture saves as a draft. A misheard phrase can be edited without redoing the entry. Leaving and returning resumes the draft. Failed publication preserves it and reports whether anything went live.

A helper assists

Geof’s source → Helper prepares draft and credit → Request review → Geof approves this revision → Authorized helper publishes.

Requested changes return to editing. Any edit after approval invalidates approval. Lack of publishing permission provides a handoff to an authorized person. Authorship and assistance remain distinct.

Correct or restore

Published item → Edit a new draft → Review → Publish correction; or Recent changes → Restore previous publication.

The live version remains visible while a correction is drafted. Undoing a first publication returns the item to a draft. Record restoration as a new event, preserving history.

Find older work

Archive → Search or filter by type → Work detail → Original or accessible reading version.

No results offers clear filters and browsing. Missing originals retain descriptive records and provenance. Restore browsing position after reading.

Family acts

Sign in → Start here → Choose a need → Read instructions → Open the existing secure system.

An external account may require its own sign-in. Denied access explains whom to contact without revealing private content. Technical access alone does not unlock family references.

Ask about teaching

Screenwriting → Feedback or classes → Understand the offer → Send an inquiry → Receive confirmation.

Begin with inquiry. Scheduling, payments, private screenplay uploads, and course delivery require later service decisions; do not imply they are available now.

Interaction rule: Every step makes the next action, current state, and way back clear. Voice, keyboard, touch, and assisted use should share the same underlying workflow.

First review with Geof

  1. Find a Parkinson’s entry and the contact route.
  2. Prepare a short update, leave it, and resume.
  3. Review a helper’s draft and ask for a correction.
  4. Approve, publish, then undo or correct it.

Observe where he hesitates, how much interaction is comfortable, whether read-aloud helps, and whether the state is clear. Confirm preferred input methods and helpers’ actual permissions before connecting accounts.
