The direction

Refine the tools that already work. Make everyday tasks easier to find, easier to complete, and easier to return to.

One familiar structure

Home

What needs attention now, ordered by importance. Cards reflect their current state and clear when resolved. A clear Home means things are running well.

My Day

Today's appointments and schedule, with a clear distinction between your own day and activity across the shop.

Business

People, shop locations, boothrent, services, hours, days off, and access in one easy-to-find place. Messages stay within reach.

Overview

How all your businesses are doing, with separate barber service earnings and shop boothrent income. Explore each shop and its detail.

What guides the work

  • Give the phone the full owner experience. Larger screens provide more room when it helps.
  • Make importance visible: very important, somewhat important, and nowhere near as important. Show progress and state separately.
  • Keep your place when you close a detail or finish a task, including the selected shop, search, and filters.
  • Make everyday work easier: finding a barber for a Re-Up, adjusting boothrent, reading announcements, and managing availability.
  • Preserve the useful reporting and management tools already in place.
Try Home → Review boothrent → Mark paid → Re-Up. The concept uses fictional people and sample data; no live payments or accounts are changed.
