Shop Policies

Know before you go. A few clear expectations can make every appointment easier.

Each barber or shop chooses only the policies that apply. Customers see a short, readable summary—not a long policy document.

THE IDEAChoose rules → add details → show customers. Start as optional information on a barber or shop profile. Connect important booking terms later.

Possible policies

A menu to choose from—not rules imposed by The BarberGame. Each selected item can include a short detail such as a time, amount, or instruction.

Appointments

  • Cancellation notice (12, 24, or 48 hours)
  • Late-arrival grace period
  • No-shows and missed appointments
  • Rescheduling and same-day changes

Payments

  • Deposit or prepayment required
  • Accepted forms of payment
  • Refunds and service corrections
  • Extra charges or variable pricing

At the shop

  • Walk-ins or appointments only
  • Guests and children
  • Pets and service animals
  • Accessibility and accommodations

Before you arrive

  • Clean or dry hair requirements
  • Consultations or preparation
  • Check-in and arrival instructions
  • Parking or entrance details

Services

  • Choose the right service and duration
  • Additional time for certain services
  • Service-specific restrictions

Personal instructions

  • One short custom note from the shop or barber
  • Preferred communication or other helpful expectations

How it could look at three shops

The same “Know Before You Go” section can be short and different for every business.

Example 01 / Appointment-based

The Appointment Studio

A busy shop with scheduled visits.

Know Before You Go
  • Cancel 24 hours ahead. Please reschedule at least one day before your visit.
  • 10-minute grace period. Later arrivals may need to rebook.
  • No-show policy. Missed appointments may be charged the full service amount.

Example only · Not a real shop's stated policy

Example 02 / Neighborhood walk-ins

The Neighborhood Shop

A casual barbershop focused on walk-ins.

Know Before You Go
  • Walk-ins welcome. Service is first come, first served.
  • Children welcome. A parent or guardian should remain present.
  • Cash and cards accepted. Ask about payment before service.

Example only · Not a real shop's stated policy

Example 03 / Specialty services

The Private Chair

Longer appointments with individual preparation.

Know Before You Go
  • Consultation first. Please choose a consultation for new specialty services.
  • Arrive with clean, dry hair. Unless your appointment includes washing.
  • Guests by arrangement. Let us know if you need assistance or accommodations.

Example only · Not a real shop's stated policy

Important distinction. Helpful instructions can simply be displayed. Deposits, cancellation charges, and other financial terms should be shown clearly before a booking is confirmed; any enforcement needs legal and payment-provider review.

Suggested next step: Agree on the first 10–15 choices with participating barbers and shop owners, then prototype a simple selection editor with an instant customer preview.
