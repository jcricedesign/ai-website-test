Shop Policies Exploration

Help clients know what to expect before they book or arrive—without making barbers write a lengthy policy document.

Opportunity

Every barber and shop operates differently. Cancellation windows, late arrivals, deposits, guests, hair preparation and practical arrival instructions are often communicated verbally, buried on a website, or discovered too late.

Recommendation: Add an optional “Know Before You Go” section to barber and shop profiles: a short list of selectable rules and helpful tips, with editable details and room for a custom instruction.

What we found

Booking platforms including Booksy, Square Appointments and Fresha already support business-specific policies such as cancellation windows, no-show fees, deposits and pre-appointment instructions. The opportunity for The BarberGame is to make these expectations easier to discover and understand across shops—even when booking happens elsewhere.

The starting example: a shop requires 24-hour cancellation notice and charges 100% of the scheduled service after a no-call/no-show, before allowing another booking. This should appear as a clear client-facing statement, not just a block of policy text.

Suggested policy menu

Barbers select only what applies. Where a rule needs a number or condition, the editor offers simple choices and a custom option.

Appointments

Cancellation notice (12/24/48 hours); late-arrival grace period; no-show fees; rescheduling.

Payments

Deposits and prepayment; accepted payment methods; refund and correction procedures.

Shop expectations

Walk-ins or appointments only; additional guests; children; pets and service animals.

Before your visit

Clean or dry hair; consultation requirements; arrive early; check-in and parking instructions.

Service details

Choose the correct service; variable prices; extra time; accessibility and entry details.

Custom instructions

One short, optional shop- or barber-specific tip that preset options do not cover.

Recommended experience

01 / Barber setup

Pick rules

Select items from a short checklist, set a notice period or fee where needed, and preview exactly what clients will read.

02 / Shop or barber profile

Read at a glance

Show a compact “Know Before You Go” list before clients commit to a visit.

03 / Booking checkout

Confirm important terms

Repeat relevant fees and cancellation terms before booking; capture acknowledgment where appropriate.

Client preview · Know Before You Go

  • 24-hour cancellation notice. Cancel or reschedule at least one day ahead.
  • 10-minute late-arrival grace period. A late appointment may need to be rescheduled.
  • No-show fee. A missed appointment may incur a fee equal to the full service price.
  • No additional guests. Please arrive alone unless assistance is needed.
  • Arrive with clean hair. Unless your selected service includes a wash.

Illustrative rules, not defaults or policies adopted by The BarberGame.

Important distinction

Helpful tips (parking, guests, hair preparation, accessibility) can be shown without formal acceptance. Financial or contractual policies (cancellations, no-show charges, deposits, refunds) should be shown clearly before confirmation; if enforced through our booking system, retain the policy version accepted with the booking.

Allow shop-wide rules to apply by default while supporting authorized barber-specific exceptions. Clearly indicate whose policy governs each appointment. Financial policies should be checked against applicable law and payment-provider requirements before enforcement.

Recommended next steps

  1. 01

    Agree on the initial list

    Morgan and a few participating shop owners review the categories. Identify the 10–15 most useful rules and any missing expectations.

  2. 02

    Prototype the selection editor

    Checkboxes, straightforward dropdowns, a short custom instruction, and a live client preview. No long-form document authoring required.

  3. 03

    Add public display to profiles

    Introduce “Know Before You Go” on shop and barber pages. Start as informational content without automated fee collection.

  4. 04

    Connect policies to booking later

    Surface relevant terms in checkout and confirmations; decide ownership, acknowledgment records, dispute handling and payment enforcement before enabling charges.

  5. 05

    Validate with real clients

    Check whether customers notice and understand the rules, and whether barbers can configure them in minutes.

Proposed first release: A voluntary policy library, configurable values, one custom tip, and a compact public summary. Avoid fee enforcement or a complex legal-policy builder in the pilot.
