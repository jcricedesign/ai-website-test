What we're building

The Washington directory is the reference dataset for customer shop discovery and later booking experiences. Our Bronze standard captures the shop name, type, address, phone, website, published hours when confirmed, a short description, highlights, source URL, provenance, status, and coordinates when available.

Public business facts are kept distinct from verified operating status. New corporate-directory imports are staged as entered / awaiting confirmation rather than treated as verified shops.
