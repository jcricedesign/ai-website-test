My Day v0.2

Research synthesis and working product model. Three perspectives are preserved rather than averaged: product/UX, barber/business, and engineering/systems.
Working thesis
My Day manages usable time. It understands who has the barber's attention now, who needs it next, and what time is available between them. When reality changes, establish what is true, understand the consequence, then offer only useful actions.

The operating loop

Business feeds future time. My Day operates present time. Completed work becomes evidence and meaning.
