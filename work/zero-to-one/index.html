0→1 Product Design

I like the part before the answer is obvious.

I’m a product designer who works best in the early stages of a product—when the opportunity is interesting, the technology is promising, and what should actually be built is still unclear.

Turning ambiguity into something a team can build.

0→1 work rarely begins with clean requirements. It starts with questions: Who is this really for? What problem are we solving? What does the technology make possible? What should we test first?

My work spans product strategy, interaction design, prototyping, and visual systems. I’m most useful when those things haven’t separated into different jobs yet.

The medium keeps changing. The underlying work doesn’t.
